### Deal C: Complete Digital Transformation (High — ₲₲₲)
**What:** Everything in B + B2B wholesale portal + Argentina site
**Scope:**

**Why they need it:** 2,300 wholesale clients is a massive untapped digital channel. Those clients currently call/WhatsApp to order — a portal lets them order 24/7, increasing order frequency and reducing administrative overhead.

**For whom:** If budget is tight but they want everything

## 3. THE PITCH FLOW

### Step 1: Open with value (not sales)
"Leticia, we built something to show you what's possible for Superspuma online. Can I walk you through it?"

### Step 2: Show, don't tell
Open https://superspuma.paragu-ai.com on your phone. Let her experience it.
- Point out: product images loaded fast, store locator with GPS, comparison table
- Then open their current site on another tab — let the contrast speak

### Step 3: Ask questions
- "What's working well with your current digital presence?"
- "What frustrates you about the current website?"
- "Where do most of your customer inquiries come from?"
- "Do your wholesale clients have a way to order online?"

### Step 4: Present the opportunity
Reference the opportunity analysis:
- Each customer can upsell +Gs. 1.17M (base + protector + almohada + cubre)
- 2,300 wholesale clients = digital ordering saves admin time
- Argentina subsidiary has no online presence at all
- Self-produced foam is a differentiator nobody talks about on their site

### Step 5: Close
- "What would make sense as a first step for you?"
- "Do you want to start with the website and see how it goes?"
- "Can I introduce myself to José Campuzano as well?"

## 4. TALKING POINTS ABOUT THE WEBSITE

### What to Highlight
- **Speed:** Our site loads in <1 second (their WordPress: 3-5 seconds)
- **Mobile:** Our site looks great on phone (the primary device in PY)
- **Images:** We took photos from their own site and made them look better
- **Catalog:** Every product has its own page with description, price, WhatsApp link
- **Stores:** Every store has GPS — customer clicks and opens Google Maps
- **Their story:** We included the Argentina subsidiary, Don Cabral partnership — shows we did our homework

### What NOT to Say
- ❌ "Your current site is terrible" (defensive)
- ✅ "Here's an idea that could improve the experience for your customers"
- ❌ "You should fire your current developer"
- ✅ "We can complement what you already have"
- ❌ "This will cost X" (without first understanding their needs)
