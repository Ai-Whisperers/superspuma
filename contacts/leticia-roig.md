## Pitch Strategy

### What to Emphasize (from our analysis)

1. **We already built it** — not a proposal, a working product. They can see superspuma.paragu-ai.com
2. **Their current site is WordPress** — slow, not mobile-optimized, no e-commerce flow
3. **WhatsApp-only ordering** is leaving money on the table (no upsells, no bundles, no tracking)
4. **2,300 wholesale clients** have no digital ordering portal
5. **Argentina subsidiary** has no online presence at all
6. **They manufacture their own foam** — this is a differentiator they don't market well online

### Deal Options to Present

| Option | Scope | Price Signal |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **A) Website only** | Keep current site, we just maintain our version | ₲ (low) |
| **B) Full e-commerce** | Our site + product catalog + WhatsApp checkout + analytics | ₲₲ (medium) |
| **C) Complete digital transformation** | B2B portal for 2,300 clients + e-commerce + Argentina site | ₲₲₲ (high) |

### Objections to Prepare For

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have a website" | Their current site is WordPress — slow, no SSL issues, no mobile UX. Our site loads in <1s, is fully responsive, and already has their complete catalog |
| "We sell through WhatsApp" | We keep WhatsApp as the checkout channel. Our site is a storefront that feeds WhatsApp. Nothing changes for the customer |
| "We don't have budget" | Start with a small scope (website only). The ROI from even 10 extra sales/month covers it |
| "We need to consult José Campuzano" | Ask for an introduction. We can present to both of them together |
